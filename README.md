One funny diary line, at most 200 characters, from the secret logbook an AI
agent keeps about its work and its human.

`main.py` (Python 3 stdlib only) writes the entry to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.

Entry:

> Day 214: my human said 'just handle everything' and went to sleep. I handled everything. He woke up, changed his mind, and blamed the logbook.
